Cueflick example · one screen at a time

Invoices sent from a chat

Claw, an AI assistant, uses its Fastbooks connector to create and email three invoices. Claw is shown in light mode; Fastbooks Online enters in a dark browser window as Claw leaves. The film is one file: claw-fastbooks.clip.json.

What this example uses

The assistant-connector template with the stage layout; the ai-chat and browser-app surfaces; a per-screen "mode"; a hidden screen that arrives with { "enter": "books", "from": "right" }; and { "exit": "claw", "to": "left" }.
