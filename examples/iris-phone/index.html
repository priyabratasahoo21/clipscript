Cueflick example · motion graphics

Less noise, more now

Glossy spheres and blocks carry the story from scene to scene. Notifications pile up on a phone, Iris folds them into one summary, music plays in the Screen Pool, and the day's few important things land as blocks. The film is one file: iris-phone.clip.json.

What this example uses

The phone-feature template; the phone surface with "material": "glass"; "actors" (spheres, and a group of labelled blocks); spawn, move with "path": "bounce" and "perch": true, leave, and confetti steps; a "backdrop" per chapter; notify, ask, summarize, and pool steps; and "cursor": false in the design.
